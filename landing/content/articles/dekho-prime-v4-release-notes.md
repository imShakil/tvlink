## What's New in v4.3.0

### 1. Arena Clash Cards for Live Sports
Live sports streaming gets an entire redesign. The new Arena Clash format displays:
- Real-time team badges and national emblems
- Match kickoff countdowns and localized Bangladeshi time (BST)
- Direct instant jump links to official live streaming channels
- Scoreboard and match status updates

### 2. Smart Battery Zero-Drain Alerts
Never miss an ICC Cricket World Cup, FIFA tournament, or continental clash. Our new background worker operates on low-power standby:
- Granular toggles for Cricket, Football, and Major Tournaments
- Zero background battery drain
- High-priority instant kick-off reminders

### 3. Android TV & Leanback Stream Health Telemetry
On Android TV and Google TV, stability is king:
- Real-time stream health and latency monitoring
- Automatic failover to backup servers when stream degradation occurs
- D-pad responsive channel surfing with numeric channel jumping support
